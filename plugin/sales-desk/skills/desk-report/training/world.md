Rinconada suits buyers who want neighborhood restaurants and access to a sheltered swimming cove. The project has no beachfront frontage or private beach access. Building 1 faces the arrival garden, building 2 the quiet east garden, building 3 the palapa courtyard, building 4 the west planting strip, and building 5 the pool garden.
