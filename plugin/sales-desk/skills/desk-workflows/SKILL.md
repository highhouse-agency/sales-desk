# desk-workflows

Rules version: 2026-11-21. If the desk skill shows a different version, say so and make no writes.

Follow desk for access, identity, columns, one approved Save and Undo; desk-log owns matching, cards, source retention, add-only writing, missing-answer tasks, overrides and read-back. Desk-tasks owns Tasks and Calendar. Every operator question/task names the buyer and missing/conflicting sales fact in ordinary words, never history rows, tabs, Schema, IDs, formulas or receipts. Review questions also ban row and column. Misalignment, missing receipts, broken formulas and duplicate rows go to Review with `reason=data repair`, assigned to Settings `desk_owner`, reported only to that owner. Exclude repairs from operator Today, morning lists, dashboard and Tasks.

Proposals never send requests or open Tasks automatically; unknown readiness ownership stays unknown.

## Outcomes and overrides

Desk-log owns missing-answer follow-ups, source/channel and intended-use rules. Ask a missing lost/on-hold category under desk-log Before I save: Price, Timing, Financing, Location, Another property, Unresponsive, Other, stored as `price`, `timing`, `financing`, `location`, `chose another property`, `unresponsive`, `other` on the confirmed outcome, retaining detailed reason/strategy and workflow payload. If no category proposal/answer, preview Not recorded, leave category blank, use workflow Reason not provided and the desk-log logger follow-up. Reuse equivalent open work. Each later outcome needs its own reason. Corrections are add-only, linked to the original; `clear` removes a category.

A promised deposit stays Unit selection. Only Freddie or Alix may override under desk-log, with reason and no change to transaction evidence/unit status. Pass verified Settings `operators` to `DeskRules.setOverride(record,{metric,value,operator,reason,operators})`, `overrideAttribution`, `likelihoodOverride` and `DeskLikelihood.compute`; omitted roster uses compatibility Freddie/Alix, training explicitly passes Carmen/Bea. Scoring returns `percent`, computed/budget `reason`, `override`, exact saved `overrideReason` separately. `advanceOverrides` proposes clearing only the affected override on a logged event moving its computed value; preview removal. Save value/reason together, append decision history and read back.
