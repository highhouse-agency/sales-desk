# One tour checklist

Use the same buyer brief and confirmed records through the visit. Capture natural updates once; do not ask for known answers again. This checklist organizes work, not evidence that a message or Calendar event was delivered.

| Step | Use existing evidence / what remains |
| --- | --- |
| Before booking | Confirm buyer identity and supplied contacts; reuse preferred_channel, interest, budget and purchase timing. Capture a supplied walk-in source separately from note transport. Review qualification and prompt for missing readiness facts before offering a tour; unknown answers stay unknown and no hard threshold is invented. Confirm the actual vetting touch, readiness and Contact and discovery stage separately. |
| Preparation | Record visit type, actual date/time with timezone, confirmed sales owner, buyer questions and any agreed visit arrangements. Store a neutral checklist/context in tour preparation, without contacts or raw messages. Jorge coverage needs an explicit agreement and does not replace Freddie as buyer owner. |
| Confirmation | Preview the booking, Tour stage for an earlier active buyer, confirmed owned host task and linked Calendar event together. Keep appointment-local date/time and zone explicit, preserving the stored instant. Reuse the host opening receipt on retries. Prepare Freddie-led personal drafts with verified host availability or ask for suitable times; never invent slots. Sending stays off until its pilot. Require separate history, Task/current-record and Calendar read-back. |
| Day-of reminder | Show the confirmed appointment and preparation work that must start today. Sending time, sender and queue require their actual approved policy; do not invent a reminder schedule or say sent. |
| Change or missed visit | Distinguish request, booked visit, reschedule, cancellation and no-show. Reschedule the same linked host task and Calendar event; cancellation closes only that visit's host work. Direct host-task cancellation asks whether the visit is also cancelled. Task-only leaves the booking and shows its hosting gap. No-show cannot precede the scheduled instant; same-day evidence needs actual time and zone. Record supplied reason and next owned action. |
| Completion and follow-up | Confirm the visit happened after its scheduled instant and no later than the actual/practice clock. Same-day completion needs its actual time; correct an incorrectly dated appointment explicitly first. Retain verified completion even if later task/Calendar/recap work fails and display the missing effect. Ask how it went and store the supplied recap with an owned dated next action, reuse confirmed work, or record an explicit no-action reason. |
| Day end | Surface named completed visits missing recap, unaccepted handoffs and proposed follow-up not yet saved. Show actual responsible owner and original due date. A chat check is available; a separate evening background prompt remains unconfigured. |

Existing approved nurture is coordinated with personal follow-up. A confirmed booking requests pause/cancellation of conflicting invitations; cancellation remains pending until the actual provider verifies it. Opt-out always wins. Do not enroll from a tour or acknowledgment alone.
